P A R T 1
People can find it

CHECKS 01 TO 07

01 Meta title perpage
WHAT IT IS The name of each page that shows in the browser tab and as the

blue link on Google.

CHECK Open three different pages and read the text on the browser tab. If
they all say the same thing, or it says something like "Vite + React"
or the name of your builder, it fails.

P R O M P T Give every page on this site its own unique meta title, under 60 characters, that
describes what that page is about and includes the business name at the end.

02 Meta description perpage
WHAT IT IS The two lines of grey text under your link on Google. It's what gets

people to click.

CHECK Paste your link into a free preview checker like metatags.io. If the

description is blank or the same on every page, it fails.

P R O M P T Write a unique meta description for every page, under 155 characters, that says

what the visitor gets from that page in plain language.

03 Open graph image
WHAT IT IS The preview picture that shows up when your link gets shared on

WhatsApp, LinkedIn or iMessage.

CHECK Send your own link to yourself on WhatsApp. If there's no image, or

it's a random cropped one, it fails.

P R O M P T Add open graph and Twitter card tags to every page, with a 1200 by 630 preview
image, a title and a description. Create a simple branded default image if one
doesn't exist.

04 Robots.txt
WHAT IT IS A tiny file that tells Google which parts of your site it's allowed to

look at.

CHECK Type yoursite.com/robots.txt into your browser. You should see a
few lines of plain text. If you get an error page it fails. If you see
"Disallow: /" on its own, that's worse, because it's telling Google to
ignore your whole site.

robots.txt

P R O M P T Add a robots.txt file that allows search engines to crawl all public pages, blocks

any admin or private routes, and links to the sitemap.

05 Sitemap.xml
WHAT IT IS A list of every page on your site so Google can actually find them all.
CHECK Type yoursite.com/sitemap.xml. You should see a list of your page

links. An error page means it fails.
P R O M P T Generate a sitemap.xml that includes every public page and updates automatically

when I add new pages.

TIP After that, add your site to Google Search Console (it's free) and submit the sitemap there.

06 Alttext on every image
WHAT IT IS A written description of each image. Screen readers read it out, and

Google uses it to understand the picture.

CHECK Ask your AI tool, "List every image on this site that has no alt text." If

the list isn't empty, it fails.

alt=

P R O M P T Add clear, descriptive alt text to every image on the site. Describe what's
actually in the image. Leave the alt empty for images that are purely decorative.

07 Analytics installed
WHAT IT IS The thing that tells you how many people visit, where they came

from and what they clicked.

CHECK Can you log into a dashboard right now and see today's visitors? If

not, it fails.
P R O M P T Install [Google Analytics 4 / PostHog / Plausible] on every page and tell me

exactly what I need to sign up for and where to paste my ID.
TIP Then open your site on your phone and check you show up in the live view.

People trustit

CHECKS 08 TO 14

08 Custom domain withSSL
WHAT IT IS Your own web address with the padlock next to it.
CHECK Look at your address bar. If it ends in something like vercel.app or

lovable.app, or the browser says "not secure", it fails.

FIX Buy the domain (usually around £10 a year), then connect it in your
hosting settings. Most hosts add the padlock for free automatically
once the domain is connected.

https: /yoursite

P R O M P T Walk me through connecting my custom domain to this project step by step, and make

sure every page redirects to the https version.

09 Favicon set
WHAT IT IS The little icon in the browser tab, and the icon people see if they

save your site to their phone.

CHECK Look at the tab. If it's a blank globe or your builder's logo, it fails.
P R O M P T Create a full favicon set from my logo, including the browser tab icon, the Apple

touch icon and the Android icons, and link them all properly.

10 Custom 404 page
WHAT IT IS What people see when they land on a link that doesn't exist.
CHECK Type yoursite.com/asdfgh. If you get a plain white error screen, it

fails.

404

P R O M P T Create a custom 404 page that matches the site design, says the page can't be
found in a friendly way, and has a clear button back to the homepage.

11 Real contact address
WHAT IT IS A real way to reach a real business. It's one of the first things people

look for before they buy.

CHECK Scroll to your footer. Is there an email on your own domain, and a
business name and address? If it's just a form with nothing else, it
fails.

P R O M P T Add a footer to every page with the business name, contact email and business

address, and a contact page with the same details.

WORTH KNOWING If you're a UK limited company, you're legally required to show your registered company name,
company number and registered office address on your site.

12 Privacy policy page
WHAT IT IS The page that tells people what data you collect and what you do
with it. You need one the second you collect an email address.
CHECK Is there a link in the footer, and does the page mention the things

your site actually does (forms, analytics, payments)?

P R O M P T Look at everything this site collects, including forms, analytics, cookies and
payments, and draft a privacy policy that covers each one in plain English. Link
it in the footer.

NOTE Treat what it writes as a first draft. If you handle payments or anything sensitive, get it looked over
properly.

13 Terms page
WHAT IT IS The rules for using your site or buying from you, including things like

refunds.

CHECK Is there a link in the footer?

P R O M P T Draft a terms page for this site covering what the service is, payments and
refunds, acceptable use and liability, in plain English. Link it in the footer.

NOTE Same as above, it's a first draft.

14 Cookie banner
WHAT IT IS The popup asking permission before you track someone.
CHECK Open your site in a private or incognito window. If you're running
analytics or ads and no banner appears, it fails. It also fails if there's
an Accept button but no equally easy way to say no.
P R O M P T Add a cookie consent banner with Accept and Reject buttons that are equally easy
to click. Make sure analytics and tracking scripts only load after someone
accepts.

WORTH KNOWING In the UK and EU you generally need consent before tracking people, so this one isn't optional
if you've done number 7.
NOTE Since February 2026 the UK lets some purely statistical analytics run without consent, as long as you
explain it and offer an easy opt out. Ads and other tracking still need consent, and so does the EU, so a banner
is still the safe default.

P A R T 3
It actually converts

CHECKS 15 TO 20

15 CTA above the fold
WHAT IT IS The main button telling people what to do, visible before they scroll.
CHECK Open your homepage on your phone and don't touch it. If you can't

see a button, it fails.
P R O M P T Make sure the homepage has one clear call to action button visible without

scrolling on both mobile and desktop.

16 Mobile breakpoints
WHAT IT IS How the layout rearranges itself for smaller screens. Most of your

visitors are on a phone.

CHECK Open every page on your phone. If you can scroll sideways, text

overlaps, or buttons are too small to tap, it fails.
P R O M P T Go through every page at phone, tablet and desktop widths. Fix anything that
overflows, overlaps or is too small to tap, and make sure nothing scrolls
sideways.

17 Sticky mobile CTA
WHAT IT IS A button that stays on screen while people scroll on their phone.
CHECK Scroll halfway down your page on mobile. If the button has gone, it

fails.
P R O M P T Add a sticky call to action bar at the bottom of the screen on mobile only, using
the same main button as the homepage. Make sure it doesn't cover any content or
the cookie banner.

18 Loading states
WHAT IT IS The spinner or "sending" message after someone clicks, so they

know it's working.

CHECK Click every button that submits or loads something. If nothing
changes for a second or two, it fails. People will click again and you'll
get duplicates.
P R O M P T Add a loading state to every button and form that takes time to respond. Disable

the button while it's loading so it can't be clicked twice.

19 Form error states
WHAT IT IS A message telling people what they got wrong.
CHECK Submit your form empty, then again with "abc" as the email. If it just
does nothing, or says "error" without saying where, it fails.

abc

P R O M P T Add validation to every form. Show a clear message next to the exact field that's
wrong, explain how to fix it, and keep everything the person has already typed.

20 Thankyou page
WHAT IT IS The page people land on after they sign up or buy.
CHECK Fill in your form properly. If you stay on the same page with a tiny

message, it fails. It needs its own address, like yoursite.com/thank-
you, because that's how your analytics counts a conversion.

/thank-you

P R O M P T Create a thank you page with its own URL that people are sent to after submitting

a form. Confirm what happens next and when they'll hear from me.

P A R T 4
Neverdo this again

Once your current site passes, you're gonna wanna make sure the next one ships like this from the
start. Paste the block below into your CLAUDE.md file in Claude Code, your rules file in Cursor, or the
project knowledge section in Lovable.

P A S T E T H I S I N
Every website in this project must ship with all of the following.
Before telling me a site is finished, check each one and list
anything that's missing.
Findable:
- Unique meta title and meta description on every page
- Open graph image, title and description on every page
- robots.txt and an auto updating sitemap.xml
- Alt text on every meaningful image
- Analytics installed and only firing after cookie consent
Trust:
- Full favicon set
- Custom 404 page that matches the design
- Footer with business name, contact email and address
- Privacy policy and terms pages linked in the footer
- Cookie banner with equal Accept and Reject options
- All pages served over https
Conversion:
- One clear CTA visible without scrolling
- Layout tested at phone, tablet and desktop widths
- Sticky CTA on mobile
- Loading states on every button and form
- Clear error messages on every form field
- Thank you page with its own URL after every form


P R O M P T
Audit this site against the checklist in my rules file. Give me a table of all 20 items
showing pass or fail, then fix every fail one at a time and show me what you changed.
